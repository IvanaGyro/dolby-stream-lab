Dolby Stream Lab

Check what this browser reports for Dolby Vision (every profile), HDR and Dolby Atmos, then play or download any single rendition from public Apple and Dolby reference streams.

1. Browser capability checks

These are what the browser claims through MediaSource.isTypeSupported() and navigator.mediaCapabilities.decodingInfo(). A claim is not proof of correct playback; use section 2 to verify.

Display

Dolby Vision, by profile

Same probe as Dolby's OptiView demo (4K60, transfer function in brackets), extended to every profile and to protected playback (PlayReady SL3000, Widevine software, FairPlay). When Dolby Vision is not decoded, cross-compatible profiles fall back to a base layer, listed in the next section.

HDR10 / HLG / SDR video (Dolby Vision base layers)

Plain video without Dolby Vision. Cross-compatible Dolby Vision streams play one of these when Dolby Vision itself is not decoded.

Dolby Atmos and Dolby audio

Same probe as Dolby's OptiView Atmos demo. Browsers cannot report whether the connected speakers or headphones are Atmos-capable.

2. Stream test

Downloads always fetch the full rendition. "Video + audio" merges both tracks into one MP4 in your browser, without re-encoding.
